# TWENTY STINGERS · CLAUDE · ROUND ONE

**Run against `prompt-stinger-round-1.md`, 2026-08-27.** Fable's and ChatGPT's twenty go in their own files. **All sixty get collated blind and shuffled before Stephen reads them.**

**1 · The Second Voicemail**
A woman left two messages the night she died, and the one her family played at the funeral was the second one; nobody has ever asked who deleted the first.

**2 · He Kept the Receipts**
For nine years a man has paid for a storage unit in the name of the woman he was cleared of killing, and last month he stopped.

**3 · Six Hours, No Adult**
Everything anyone knows about that night comes from a frightened fourteen-year-old at four in the morning, and he has spent the twenty years since trying to take it back.

**4 · Nobody's Daughter**
A girl was buried under a number twenty years ago and three women have since told the same police station she was theirs; two of them were lying and one was not.

**5 · The Woman in the Photograph**
Her husband reported her missing on a Tuesday and by Friday he had chosen the photograph the papers would use, and it is not one she would ever have picked.

**6 · Day Thirty-Two**
A hotel kept its recordings for a month, the family asked for them a day too late, and the man who told them they were gone had already watched them.

**7 · The Man Who Waited**
He sat with her family every day of the trial, brought them food, and cried when the verdict came in; he had killed her in March.

**8 · Learn to Swim**
A boy who had swum since he was six drowned in water he could stand up in, and the only adult on the bank has changed his account four times.

**9 · Her Own Handwriting**
The note that closed the case was in her handwriting, which is why nobody looked at the fact that she had written it eleven months before she died.

**10 · The Other Family**
He was buried by his wife and children on the Thursday; the woman who came on the Friday had been married to him for nineteen years and is the only one asking how he died.

**11 · The Dress She Was Wearing**
She left the house in a green dress and was found in a blue one, and the person who noticed was not the police.

**12 · Two Sisters, One Story**
They gave identical statements about the night their mother died, word for word, and neither of them has spoken to the other since.

**13 · The Coach**
Forty families let him drive their children home, and when one boy did not come back, every one of them said the same three words about him.

**14 · Sold as Seen**
The house went to auction eight weeks after she died in it, and the man who bought it is the one who found her.

**15 · She Told Her Hairdresser**
She told one person she was frightened and it was not her sister, her doctor or the police, and that person has been waiting nine years for someone to ask.

**16 · Who Signed Him Out**
He finished work at ten and was found on the same site at dawn, and the man who signed him out that night has never once been asked why he did.

**17 · A Very Good Liar**
The only witness was eight years old and everyone agreed she was too young to be believed, and she is thirty now and remembers all of it.

**18 · The Wrong Brother**
Two brothers were in the house, one is dead, one is serving life, and their mother has spent twelve years refusing to say which of them she was protecting.

**19 · Their Own Key**
She was twenty-six, she lay in a flat above a shop that opens at six every morning, and the person who eventually let themselves in had their own key.

**20 · He Came Back for the Dog**
The house was on fire, he got out, and he went back in for the dog and not for her.

# TWENTY MORE · 21 TO 40

**Second batch, same prompt, 2026-08-27.** Checked against the first twenty so that no two turn on the same central question.

**21 · The Emergency Call**
He rang for an ambulance four minutes before she fell down the stairs, and nobody noticed for nine years.

**22 · Interviewed as a Witness**
The man who found her was questioned for twenty minutes as a helpful neighbour and never again, and he had washed his car at two in the morning.

**23 · She Was Already Packing**
Her suitcase was open on the bed with three days of clothes in it, and everyone agreed she had no plans to go anywhere.

**24 · The Wrong Person Was Told**
His mother learned he was dead from a woman she had never met, who had known for two days, and who has never explained how.

**25 · Every Time It's Mentioned**
He has moved house four times in eleven years and each move came within a month of somebody publicly asking about the girl from the caravan park.

**26 · The Dog Wouldn't Go In**
She died in the kitchen, the family stayed in that house another two years, and the only one who never walked into that room again was the dog.

**27 · A Twin Would Know**
She identified her sister's body in four seconds and told them it was wrong, and they wrote down that she was too distressed to look properly.

**28 · Nobody Believed Him**
A man told three people he had killed her, and all three decided he was the kind of man who says things like that.

**29 · Six Weeks of Flowers**
Someone left flowers at the spot every week for six weeks and then stopped, and the week they stopped is the week the wrong man was charged.

**30 · Her Phone Kept Answering**
For six weeks after she disappeared somebody was replying to her messages, and they were good at it, and her mother did not notice.

**31 · Two Streets Apart**
Two women died two streets apart and eleven months apart, and the only person who ever said their names in the same sentence was the man who killed them.

**32 · What He Did for a Living**
Nobody looked at him for six years because of his job, and the job was the reason he knew exactly how long it would take anyone to look.

**33 · She Reported It**
She walked into a station to say her boyfriend had killed someone and walked out under arrest, and the body was found where she said it would be.

**34 · The Last Message**
Her last text used a word she had never used in her life, and her sister has spent nine years explaining that to people who nod.

**35 · He Was Never Alone**
Every account of that night puts one man on his own for eleven minutes, and he has spent twenty years insisting somebody was with him and refusing to say who.

**36 · The Wrong Language**
He confessed in a language he learned at forty and gave his account in the one he was born in, and the two do not describe the same night.

**37 · He Inherited Her Debt**
When she died her son got nothing but the money she owed, and he has spent nine years finding out who she owed it to.

**38 · The Stranger at the Funeral**
A man nobody knew stood at the back, signed the book with a name that does not exist, and has done the same at two other funerals.

**39 · She Said He'd Be Charming**
Her diary describes exactly how he would explain it if anything happened to her, and it reads like a transcript of what he said.

**40 · The Boy Who Wasn't There**
Three people put him in a room at the time she died and all three are related to him, and the fourth person in that room has never been named.
