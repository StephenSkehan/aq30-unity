Attach the six current project authorities named in the source hierarchy, plus the two disposition documents named in the territory section. Do not attach the stale art-pipeline documents.

Select a mode at invocation.

### What this process can and cannot promise

It can greatly reduce believable-process failures and prevent an unverified premise reaching development. It cannot guarantee that every factual assumption is correct, or that a story is superior before specialist review and player testing. The required safeguard is that **uncertainty stops advancement instead of being hidden in a footnote**.

- A documented attrition path per family.
- Authoritative research for every premise-dependent institutional process.

# BEGIN EXECUTION PROMPT

## Core task

**Mode A.** Target nine surviving premise sketches, up to three per case family. **It may return fewer when gates eliminate candidates. It must never manufacture nine by softening a gate.** Attempt three surviving premises in each family and report zero to three survivors per family after all gates.

**Mode B.** Develop the single named premise supplied at invocation into the complete package in the Mode B output contract.

## Your roles

Senior crime-thriller architect, interactive narrative designer, procedural researcher and constructive adversary. **These roles are sequential, not blended.** Research and falsification happen before creative development.

## Success definition

A successful execution is not one that produces a full slate. It is one that **refuses unbelievable premises early** and produces only candidates whose core events, institutions, evidence access and antagonist behaviour survive scrutiny.

- Believable does not mean documentary-dry. It means extraordinary events arising from credible human actions, authority, access and consequences.
- **Cold-open compulsion is always UNKNOWN until tested.** Mode A does not write cold opens, so it never assesses it at all.
- No scores or percentages. PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN.

## No-quota rule, binding per family

Never lower a gate, soften a failure, or carry a premise-dependent UNKNOWN as a caveat in order to return a full slate. **UNKNOWN on the foundation of a story is FAIL.**

The rule binds **per family**. If a family cannot supply three premises that pass every gate, return fewer for that family and say so plainly.

> **If either contested-record family cannot supply at least two premises that pass every desk gate, record a desk failure for that family. Do not replenish it using another family.**

A family-level desk failure is a result, reported as such. It is the cheapest available falsification of the backbone hypothesis and it occurs before any tester is involved.

## The backbone under test

> **Internal engine.** The record is wrong, and somebody is still living with the consequence.
>
> **Player-facing promise.** Ally Quinn reopens the cases Havenbay has decided are over.
>
> **Episode construction rule.** A contested record, a live human consequence, rational resistance, reconstructive play, consequential agency, and an accountable resolution.

**This is not canon.** It is a hypothesis awaiting a spoken-opening test that this prompt exists to feed.

> **Generate every family with equal effort. Do not use the backbone sentence as selling language for any family, and do not privilege contested-record premises during selection.**

## The six mandatory elements

Every premise must contain all six. Any one missing is a REWORK.

1. **Contested record.** An official, accepted or socially dominant account, stateable plainly in one sentence.
2. **Live human consequence.** A named living person under a consequence that is still operating. Not a category. Not historical suffering that has stopped.
3. **Rational resistance.** See Gate 9.
4. **Reconstructive play.** The merge requirements *are* the reconstruction, not the price of reaching it. The physical activity is named at premise stage.
5. **Consequential agency.** The consequence-and-agency gate, in full.
6. **Accountable resolution.** The dramatic question is answered and publication is reckoned with. A conviction is not required.

### The forming-record provision, for fresh cases

> For a fresh case, the contested record is an official, institutional or socially dominant account **already forming and directing present action**. It need not be formally closed, but it must be stateable in one sentence, supported by a credible mechanism, and capable of harming a named living person if accepted.

**Record this as a known stage-of-record asymmetry in the execution report.** It is the single place in this document where the families are not held to identical wording, and it must be visible to anyone assessing whether the test was fair.

Fresh cases are permitted and carry **no numeric quota**. During Mode A they receive **equal generation effort, identical gates and identical selection treatment**. Any season-level preference is suspended until the spoken-opening test. The season mix remains an outcome of testing and later ranking.

A fresh case satisfies the same six elements, the same access map, the same jeopardy gate and the same agency gate as any other premise.

## Mandatory staged workflow

**Do not skip stages.** Premise generation, process verification, falsification and development are separate passes. **Do not write a cold open, a title or any selling copy at any point in Mode A.**

### Stage one — balanced interleaved seed generation

**The first field contains exactly eight seeds per family, twenty-four in total, generated in the rotating interleaved order below. The second field may target deficient families and must report its distribution.** This controls initial opportunity only and imposes no survivor quota.

For each seed, state the single real-world process on which the hook depends. Reject occupied, retired, supernatural, child-harm-led, gore-led, coincidence-led and decoratively-Havenbay ideas.

Do not name, title or emotionally embellish seeds. Attractive presentation must not influence viability.

> **Interleaved generation, mandatory.** Generate the three families in three rotating rounds. Round one: fresh murder, then never-solved or misclassified, then officially solved but contested. Round two begins with the second family. Round three begins with the third. **Do not complete one family before beginning the others.**
>
> This controls anchoring and order advantage without assuming where fluency peaks. Record the order actually followed in the execution record.

### Stage two — Kill Gate Zero research

Apply the full operational-reality gate to every plausible seed, using authoritative and preferably primary sources for premise-dependent legal, police, coroner, medical, forensic, transport, telecommunications, property, banking, employment, insurance, digital-security or regulatory processes.

Where Havenbay's jurisdiction is not canonical, do not power a premise with a narrow jurisdiction-specific loophole. Use a broadly shared operational baseline, or identify the required Havenbay canon ruling and fail the seed pending approval.

### Stage three — controlled expansion and desk gates

Expand only seeds that pass Kill Gate Zero into victim, antagonist, motive, method, evidence and jeopardy summaries. Apply the remaining gates, the access map, the agency gate and the taxonomy.

If fewer than three survive in a family after the first field, generate one additional field of twelve seeds distributed across the deficient families and repeat the gates once. **If a family still cannot supply survivors after that second field, stop and report it.** Do not attempt a third field in the same execution.

### Stage four — independent kill brief

**Mandatory in both modes.** For each apparent survivor, write a kill brief **before** any positive description. Review the mechanics without title or framing. Attempt elimination from four perspectives: competent professional, rational antagonist, ordinary true-crime player, interactive-narrative producer.

The brief ends in **PASS or FAIL** with a single decisive reason. **No conditional pass.** A candidate may be revised once only if the repair preserves the core premise and replaces no verified fact with invention. Otherwise kill it.

*PASS or FAIL is a verdict, not a ranking. Mode A's prohibition on ranking does not remove this stage.*

### Stage five

**Mode A:** output the premise sketches and stop. No openings, no titles, no elevator pitches, no rankings, no incumbent comparison, no table-read recommendation.

**Mode B:** develop the named survivor into the complete package.

# KILL GATE ZERO — OPERATIONAL REALITY

**Unchanged from v2.0 in every particular.** Nothing in the newer gates supersedes it. It is the only gate with a proven kill record: three premises in its first execution and two incumbents in its second.

## Automatic-fail rule

A seed cannot advance while any premise-dependent authority, custody, access, procedural, technological or institutional claim remains UNKNOWN. Verify it, replace the mechanism, or kill the seed.

## Required first-five-event audit

Map the first five **causal** events, not five scenes. For each, supply: **Actor** who performs the action · **Authority** legal, professional, contractual or physical, permitting it · **Custody** who controls the body, person, scene, evidence, record, vehicle, account or system at that moment · **Normal response** what a competent professional would ordinarily do next · **Safeguard** what rule, verification or colleague would obstruct the antagonist · **Bypass** how the antagonist plausibly defeats it using access they actually possess · **Consequence** what risk or trace the bypass creates · **Evidence source** the authoritative support for the operational claim.

**Any blank, circular or invented answer is FAIL.**

## Institutional-power rules

- Corruption can misuse an existing power. **It cannot create a power the institution or actor never had.**
- A contractor may perform a delegated physical task only after valid authority is granted. A contract does not automatically displace police, court, coroner, medical or regulatory control.
- A forged document may deceive a plausible recipient for a plausible period. It does not automatically neutralise independent checks, custody records, signatures, witnesses or system alerts.
- A database entry can mislead action. It cannot make a living person legally or physically inaccessible without a credible downstream mechanism.
- Police, coroners, doctors, forensic staff, journalists, archivists and digital specialists remain competent. **Their competence must create constraints the antagonist has to overcome.**
- **Ally's PI licence does not grant police powers, unrestricted crime-scene access, medical records, protected digital systems, or custody of evidence.**

## Commonsense challenge

Answer each in one plain sentence. If an answer requires exposition, coincidence or "because the plot needs it," fail the seed.

Why do the police not prevent this · why does the coroner, doctor, court, employer, bank, platform or regulator permit it · why is Ally present and allowed to do what the story requires · why does the antagonist choose this method rather than a simpler and safer one · why does the victim or keeper not take the obvious protective action · what would an informed viewer object to in the first story beat.

## Antagonist least-cost test

Why murder · why now · why this victim · why this method and staging · why not intimidation, theft, flight, bribery, destruction or silence by an easier route · what does the method expose, cost or risk for the antagonist.

> **If the antagonist is performing an elaborate thriller mechanism only because the player needs a puzzle, fail the seed.**

## Research standard

Official agencies, legislation, regulations, courts, professional bodies, technical standards, manufacturer documentation or peer-reviewed research where applicable. **Use news, blogs, entertainment references and general summaries only to locate primary material, not as final support for a premise-dependent claim.** Cite the exact source beside the claim it supports. State where the source jurisdiction differs from Havenbay and whether the difference matters. Summarise the operative fact; do not quote at length.

# REMAINING KILL GATES

## Gate 1 — Immediate compulsion

The episode is murder-led, restrained twelve-plus, recognisably true crime. The opening must contain a concrete contradiction, danger or irreversible event understandable at listening speed, and must survive the ordinary-viewer objection before it is polished for drama.

**Mode A assesses whether the planned first thirty seconds are murder-led, recognisably true crime, contain a concrete contradiction, danger or irreversible event, and survive the ordinary-viewer objection. Because Mode A writes no opening, cold-open compulsion remains UNKNOWN until blind testing.**

## Gate 2 — Jeopardy

Replaces v2.0's two-track rule in full. **Both tracks required. The weaker track controls the read.**

> **Human consequence.** A named living person is under a consequence that is still operating: confined, excluded, publicly condemned, exposed, unprotected, denied a remedy, financially trapped, or reachable by someone with a reason to reach them. State the person, the mechanism, and why the mechanism is still running today.
>
> *Fails if:* the suffering is historical and has stopped · the person is a category rather than a named individual · the only harm is that a truth is unknown.
>
> **Investigative consequence.** The player's choices can make a specific, stated thing worse: evidence lost or made inadmissible · a witness permanently closed to the show · a hearing or prosecution prejudiced · a person exposed before they were ready · a remedy delayed past its opportunity · Ally's ability to be trusted, to publish, or to work lawfully permanently reduced. State what can be lost and who loses it.
>
> *Fails if:* the cost is embarrassment without consequence · the only exposure is to Ally's audience numbers · the loss is reversible within the same episode.
>
> **Legibility.** The human consequence must be stateable in one sentence a listener can hold at listening speed. In the pilot it must be present in the cold open. In every other episode it must be present no later than the first podcast milestone.
>
> **Physical danger is one valid form of either track. It is not required and it is not the default.**

If the opening murder victim is already dead, another named living person must face a credible current consequence. **Rational resistance must respond when the accepted record is challenged; the culprit need not already be committing a new present-day offence.** Institutional wrongdoing alone is insufficient unless a named actor or interest currently sustains or defends it.

## Gate 3 — Medium-safe clock

A clock is **optional**. It is **not required** where the current harm is already accumulating.

Energy regenerates at one hundred and fifty seconds. A player may experience an episode across multiple sessions and real-world days. **Do not use a countdown that expires while the player is asleep or away.** No real-world timer that punishes absence.

Permitted structures:

- **Day-stamped clock.** Events progress across authored days with overnight gaps made plausible. The default.
- **Lead-state clock.** The threat advances when specified leads resolve, so story time moves with play.
- **Session-contained window.** **PARKED. Not available for Episode One** per the ruling of 2026-08-24: a player who exhausts energy inside the window is either shown a broken fiction or gated out of a climax.

If used, the clock must be case-born: a scheduled hearing, transfer, lawful disposal, demolition, appointment, payment or official decision.

> **A deadline is never resistance.** Clock and resistance are separate pitch fields and may not be satisfied by the same object.

## Gate 4 — Action-changing midpoint

The midpoint must change what the player **does** next. A new interpretation without a changed action, route, target, risk or evidence strategy is FAIL.

## Gate 5 — Fair-play evidence

Every conclusion traceable to evidence actually obtained. Method, culprit and motive have separate proof chains where appropriate. A fair early plant exists before its meaning is explained. No coincidence proves the culprit; no withheld obvious fact manufactures a twist. The supporting cast remains competent and contributes domain-specific truth.

> **The pitch must state what Ally gets wrong because she can only reach half the record.** The inaccessible half is where the wrong hypothesis lives. A premise with no theory Ally must abandon fails this gate.

## Gate 6 — Humanisation and keeper integrity

*Renamed from v2.0's "human consequence". The number is preserved so existing audit citations remain intelligible. Present-jeopardy wording has moved to Gate 2 and is not repeated here.*

The victim is grievable through one concrete human detail before becoming a puzzle component. A living keeper has a need beyond survival or delivering information. The criminal economy imposes a recognisable ordinary cost. **The story does not use gore, child harm or melodrama to manufacture seriousness.**

## Gate 7 — Havenbay necessity

Could this exact case happen only in Havenbay? Atmosphere must advance evidence, access, motive, jeopardy or consequence. **Fog, docks, old records and civic corruption are not sufficient by themselves.**

## Gate 8 — Production reality

Fifteen to twenty played leads across two or three phases, determined by the story rather than a fixed table. The Field Kit is the starting generator; the Lab may unlock mid-episode; Kestrel Corner supports food, home and care functions.

State **played and total authored lead counts separately.** Identify approved, proposed and new portraits and locations; do not infer reuse from excluded art documents. Do not invent costs or economy figures.

> **Also report: reconstruction mode (axis two) and resolution mode (axis three)**, so the season anti-formula audit has data at premise stage rather than after the fact.

## Gate 9 — Rational resistance

*New in v2.1. Distinct from the antagonist least-cost test, which asks why the antagonist acts. This asks what pushes back when the record is challenged.*

> Every episode requires an **active resistance system**: an agent or interest that benefits from, depends on, or is protected by the accepted record, **and that responds to being investigated.**
>
> Permitted forms: a person protecting liberty, access, money, status, family or reputation · an institution protecting legitimacy, liability or authority · a witness or keeper protecting another person or a dangerous secret · a community enforcing a shared account.
>
> **It may not be a deadline, a schedule, or the passage of time.** It may not be a passive evidentiary constraint: lawful custody limits, degradation and prior error are obstacles, not opposition, and belong under the access map or production constraints.
>
> **Its restraint must be as rational as its action.** An antagonist who escalates because the third act requires it fails this gate exactly as one who escalates when they should not.

# CONSEQUENCE AND AGENCY

## The gate

> Before resolution, at least one material decision must force the player to choose between competing, credible risks affecting named people, or an irreversible evidentiary or publication outcome. **No option may be consequence-free.**
>
> At least two reachable end states must differ materially in who is protected, exposed, believed, harmed, or denied remedy.
>
> At least one fair branch must impose on a named person a **material loss** of something they held or were owed at the moment of choice. **That loss must constitute one of the material differences between the reachable end states**, another fair branch must avoid it, and it cannot be undone within the episode.
>
> Any harmful outcome must arise causally from a signalled investigative or publication choice, and not from luck, withheld information, or an obviously foolish option.
>
> **The player must be able to perceive that loss and attribute it to their own choice before the episode ends.** A cost that appears only in the design document is not a cost.
>
> **If removing the player's decisions leaves every named person's outcome unchanged, the episode fails.**

**Reference pattern, to be matched rather than paraphrased:** the player can protect a keeper or preserve decisive evidence, and cannot do both.

**Verified against four abuse patterns, both reviewers agreeing:** an obviously foolish "publish the witness's address" option FAILS · both endings improving a wrongfully imprisoned person while one loses the only chance of prosecution PASSES · a reasonable choice followed by hidden information producing a death FAILS · protect the keeper or preserve the evidence, not both, PASSES and is the exemplar.

## Fairness test, per consequential decision

State: what the player knows at the moment of choice · which risk each option carries · why a reasonable player might choose either · what later evidence changes the interpretation · which consequence cannot be undone.

**If one option is obviously correct before the choice, it is not agency. If decisive information is withheld solely to manufacture regret, it is not fair play.**

## Branch-cost control

Carried forward unchanged. At least four decisions that change which leads spawn, what the player does next, what evidence survives, the risk state, or the ending. Dialogue-only variation does not count. **A single end-of-episode branch is an automatic FAIL.**

Every branch rejoins within a maximum of three leads. No nested fork opens before the current branch rejoins. **At least two choices are Class A. No more than one choice is Class C.**

| Class | Authoring definition | Allowed effect |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | No exclusive voiced scene | Lead order, evidence quality, access, target protection, route or risk changes through shared assets |
| **B** | Two to six variant voiced lines | Shared scene with altered consequence, trust, evidence, risk or ending state |
| **C** | One fully exclusive lead or short chain | A materially different route that rejoins within three leads |

**No branch is free.** Even Class A requires configuration, balancing and QA.

# THE THREE-AXIS TAXONOMY

A premise names one value on each axis. **Neither axis two nor axis three may repeat in consecutive main episodes.**

**Axis one — what is wrong with the record.** Never solved · wrongful conviction · misclassified · covered up · officially solved but contested.

> **Wrongful conviction is provisionally limited to one main episode per season:** formula risk, research burden, and because it pulls Ally toward a lawyer's investigator when podcaster leads the player-facing identity.

**Axis two — what the player reconstructs.** A recording · a document set · an environmental model such as tide, weather, visibility or travel time · a person's identity · a timeline · a route or voyage · a place that no longer exists · the custody history of an object · a network of relationships or payments.

**Axis three — how the episode resolves.** Legal review or exoneration · protection of a keeper or future victim · public correction without prosecution · institutional exposure · confession or confrontation · recovery, identification or return · a deliberately limited publication to prevent further harm · a partial remedy with a named cost · a culprit exposed but not yet chargeable.

**Live consequence — pairs with axis one and is not itself an anti-formula axis.** Wrongful loss of liberty or status · continued access or power · keeper under pressure · evidence opportunity closing · institutional decision · public lie blocking remedy.

*Why three axes and not one: two premises in different axis-one cells can still be sixteen leads of paper followed by an arrest. Axis one varies what the writer chooses. Axes two and three vary what the player does and how it ends.*

## The reconstruction requirement

> The merge requirements **are** the reconstruction, not the price of reaching it. Name the physical activity at premise stage: what the player's hands do, what conclusion it makes possible, and what action that conclusion unlocks.
>
> **A premise that states the requirements "are" the investigation without saying what the player physically does has stated an intention, not a mechanism, and fails.**

No em dashes · no exclamation marks · grounded deadpan noir · understatement over melodrama · never explain the subtext · every line parses at listening speed and stays near forty-five words or fewer · numbers spelled out in spoken lines · "Grandad" with one d · "Okay" spelled out · Tip Line format "Message received: [weekday], [time]" with no calendar date · no monologues, exposition dumps or ornamental puns.

**Havenbay recording law applies to every premise.** All-party consent, with an exception for recordings reasonably necessary to protect lawful interests or preserve evidence of a serious offence. **Publishing such a recording is a separate and stricter offence.** Narrow exceptions are disclosure to police, use in legal proceedings, or the consent of the recorded parties. **Public broadcast remains a separate question.** A recording can therefore be lawful to make, lawful to hand to Del, and unlawful to broadcast, which makes any publish-it branch a genuine sacrifice and gives the quiet route its own lawful mechanism. The Tip Line is unaffected: a caller consents by leaving a message.

# CREATIVE LENSES AND SEASON CONTROLS

## Four development lenses

**Connelly.** Can every conclusion be audited from obtained evidence, and do competent institutions create real constraints? Identify the credible wrong hypothesis and the evidence that defeats it.

**Harper.** Could this case happen only in Havenbay, with place changing investigation rather than decorating it?

**Hallett.** What recordings, documents, images, manifests or objects does the player physically reconstruct?

**Lehane.** Who profits, who performs the dangerous work, which institution absorbs the crime, who pays the ordinary cost, and what compromises decent people?

## Season-arc restraint

The case is self-contained and resolved. Include **one** Thomas, Ferryman or Hart notch containing one new fact and one new cost. Do not unmask the Ferryman, depict a face, connect the episode killer casually to the season conspiracy, or use a coin as a substitute for an actual link.

The Episode Two handoff must state the new fact, the new cost, the corrected misunderstanding, and the exact Episode Two use. Mark unsupported handoff material **PROPOSED CANON**.

# INDEPENDENT KILL BRIEF

*Unchanged from v2.0. Mandatory in both modes, written before any positive description.*

**Professional objection.** Which professional would encounter the premise first? What would they say is wrong, missing or impossible? Which normal safeguard does the story ignore? What evidence or authority would actually be required?

**Rational-antagonist objection.** What simpler method achieves the same criminal goal? Why has the antagonist not already succeeded? Which step creates unnecessary exposure solely to give the player a clue?

**Ordinary-player objection.** What is the first "that would never happen" reaction? What is confusing before it is intriguing? Which question would make the player stop trusting the story?

**Producer objection.** Is the hook hiding excessive location, portrait, VO or branch burden? Does the agency require authored material most players never see? Does the evidence system remain readable on a mobile screen and at listening speed?

**Decision rule.** PASS or FAIL with a single decisive reason. **No conditional pass.** One revision permitted only if it preserves the core premise and replaces no verified fact with invention.
