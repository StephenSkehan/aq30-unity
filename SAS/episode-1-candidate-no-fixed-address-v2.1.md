# 1. THE CRIME, AND WHAT THE BODY SHOWED

Kerry Nolan, thirty-four, left Dean Iles in September and moved without telling him. She rented a room in Rivermouth and took a night cleaning shift at the Hart Civic Center. She made no complaint and sought no order against him. **She simply left, which is why there is no legal document anywhere connecting her new life to his name.**

In November her sister-in-law posted a photograph with a recognisable window behind it. He found her in nine days. He went to make her talk to him. She backed away, he took hold of her, she went down and struck her head once on the corner of the hearth.

**Cause of death: a single blunt impact to the back of the skull. No defensive injuries. No ligature. No weapon.**

He sat in his van for two hours. Then he drove her to the foot of the embankment steps below the Stonebridge Viaduct and left her there.

**Why misadventure is a competent finding.** A single impact head injury on a woman found at the foot of a flight of wet concrete steps in November is exactly what a fall down those steps produces. The pathology is unremarkable and the scene explains it. **The autopsy is not wrong and the coroner is not lazy.** The same injury on a woman found on her own hearth would have been looked at very differently, and that is the whole point: **the pathology is identical and the inference is not.** That is unequal looking, and it is the episode's mechanism rather than its excuse.

# 2. WHY SHE IS STILL UNIDENTIFIED, ROUTE BY ROUTE

The v1.0 claim that "nothing existed to make the records meet" was too absolute and contradicted its own normal-response row. Each route is now answered.

| Route | Why it produced nothing |
|---|---|
| **Fingerprints** | Taken post-mortem and searched correctly. Kerry has no criminal record and never held a role requiring printing, so **she is not on file.** A correct search of a database she was never in returns nothing |
| **Dental** | Odontology is **confirmatory, not searching.** It compares a body to a named candidate. Until a candidate exists there is nothing to compare against. This is true of real practice and is not a shortcut |
| **DNA** | Requires a reference sample. The only available reference is her mother, and **no reference was ever taken from Maureen**, for the reason in the next row |
| **The interstate report's classification** | ★ **The load-bearing repair.** Kerry was reported by her mother as an adult who had moved interstate, changed jobs, and cut contact after leaving a partner. That is correctly classified **low-risk, voluntarily absent.** Low-risk adult reports do not attract biometric reference collection, because nobody believes the person is dead. **Nobody thought Kerry needed finding. They thought she had left.** So there was never anything on Kerry's side of the comparison to match against |
| **Physical description** | Compared and produced candidates. She was among them. **A description match is not an identification**, it is a list, and a list of possible thirty-to-forty-five-year-old women requires biometric or family follow-up to resolve. See the ruling question below |
| **Automated cross-jurisdictional matching** | ★ **ANSWERED. Bible §2.3.2 R16, Stephen-ruled 2026-08-26: participation is VOLUNTARY AND PATCHY.** Ruled from real-world practice, not from what this premise needed. Kerry's record may or may not have been entered; either way **entry is not identification**, and the candidate list it produces cannot be resolved without the reference sample nobody took |

**The stay-away condition of v1.0 is deleted.** The reviewer correctly identified it as an unexplained link between Kerry's legal identity and Dean. She never made a complaint. Nothing on paper joins them.

**What this costs Maureen, and it is the theme.** The classification that said *she has probably just left* is the same classification that meant nobody took her mother's DNA. The system was not wrong about the risk. It was wrong about Kerry, and it was wrong in the direction that costs nothing to be wrong in.

# 3. ANTAGONIST, WITH THE PHASE CHANGE STATED

**Why murder.** He did not go to kill her. The killing is the failure of a visit he should not have made, which is what the overwhelming majority of these deaths are.

**Why the steps.** He needed a place where a head injury explains itself and where the person is presumed to be someone nobody is looking for. **The steps do both jobs**, which is why they are worth a twenty-minute drive and the risk of the van. This is now testable because the cause of death is written.

**PHASE ONE, leads 1 to 11: Dean does nothing.** While Kerry is unidentified he is invisible, and every action he could take would make him less so. He does not contact Maureen. He does not contact Ally. **The reviewer was right that v1.0's proactive helpfulness was irrational, and it is deleted.**

**PHASE TWO, from the lead where identification becomes likely:** Ally reaches him, because he is named in Maureen's report as the last partner. **Now cooperation is his cheapest option**: refusing would draw the attention that silence used to buy. He is warm, volunteers small true things, and manages what Ally believes about him.

**PHASE THREE, only if the player publishes early:** now that Kerry has a name, control of the story is cheaper than absence, and he contacts Maureen himself. **This is a consequence of a player choice, not a character trait**, and it is unreachable on the other branch.
