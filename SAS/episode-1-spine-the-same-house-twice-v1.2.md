**Arithmetic unchanged and still passing:** two Class A, one Class C, rejoins 3 / 2 / 3 / 1, no fork before the current branch rejoins, leads 7, 10 and 14 shared.

### Decision 1 · lead 4 · Class A

**Take it.** Josie's report is dated seven weeks before Ally existed, signed, and about a named client. It is the difference between a podcaster's theory and a professional's recorded concern, and it is what makes the official machine move faster than it otherwise would.
**Leave it with her.** Ally has only her own voice, and the machine moves at its own pace.

★ **The cost is now real and lawful:** handing a document about a client to a journalist is a data breach. **It ends Josie's registration, not just her job.** She offers it anyway.

### Decision 2 · lead 8 · Class B

★ **The false cost is gone.** Marion requesting records does not warn Colin, and no lawful person would tell him.

**Tell her.** She has standing Ally never will, and the green lamps enter the record as a daughter's account rather than a podcaster's anecdote. **Edith was cremated and the pharmacy record expired nine years ago, so Marion will spend the rest of her life on a question that has no answer available to it.**
**Let her keep the version she has.** Sixteen years of an ordinary sad death stay ordinary and sad, and **the one detail that makes the whole pattern legible never enters the record at all.**

**Irreversible:** a woman's remaining years, or the only witness detail that explains what happened.

### Decision 3 · lead 11 · Class C

★ **v1.1's deception version is deleted. Finding 3 was right: Yardley can ask honestly, and he did, on Day 3, and she said no.** The question is no longer how to get the test. It is **who asks her again.**

**Ally asks.** A stranger, a podcaster, and precisely the ghoul Colin described to her on Day 4. It confirms everything he said and it may close the door permanently.
**Marion asks.** A woman whose mother died in that house, saying it to her face, and Winnie cannot dismiss her the way she can dismiss Ally. **It also puts Marion in a room with Colin, and Marion is not able to sit opposite that man and say nothing.**

**Irreversible:** Ally's last standing with Winnie, or what Marion does in that room.

### Decision 4 · lead 15 · Class A

★ **Rebuilt on finding 7. It no longer asks police to tolerate lethal harm, because the police are already moving and Ally is choosing whether to preempt them.**

**Tell him.** He stops, he goes, **Winnie lives** — and she never believes a word of it, because the man who was kind to her vanished the week a podcaster started telling lies about him. The grave application loses its living subject and Marion's question closes with nothing in it.
**Hold two days.** The application is heard, the machine takes over, and all three are answered for. **Two more days of Colin in that house with a woman he is already poisoning, and he has no idea he needs to stop.**

**Irreversible:** two deaths never answered for and Winnie's last friendship intact but false, or the two days.

### Counterfactual

Remove every decision: everyone still acts on Day 3, Winnie still refuses, the application is still heard, and Colin is caught after she dies. ★ **The counterfactual is not a world where nobody tried. It is a world where everybody tried correctly and it did not matter, which is the episode's whole argument.**
