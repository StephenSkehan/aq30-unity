The friends. Four people, her closest, who have keys or near-keys to her life: Liam, who has known her since they were eleven and holds a spare key to her house; Ruby, a nurse, her Tuesday-morning friend, who sits in the car with her with the heater on; Margo, an accountant who does her books and knows her money; Brad, a younger signwriter she taught for seven years on Tuesday nights at her unit, who has a key to the unit. What the scenario must do. Design an event, or a small chain of events, that would make this particular woman conclude: one of those four is planning to kill me, I do not know which, and I may be wrong. Each candidate must satisfy all of the following, and you must show how:
