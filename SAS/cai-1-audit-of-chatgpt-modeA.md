# 7. One item OUT OF INSTRUMENT

Recorded, not determinative, carried forward.

Every one of the nine decisive-evidence routes runs through **a living private individual who consents**: Saira's camera, Arthur's circulation card, Lian's photographs, Hemi's notebook, Harold's counterfoils, Asha's family copies, Sam's invoice, the employer's payroll sheet, Ava's notebook. It also means the player's route to proof is **always persuading one frightened person to give up something that costs them.** Combined with the situation monoculture it produces a second-order repetition the registers do not capture: not only *the wrong person is blamed*, but *the way you help them is always to spend someone else's livelihood.* Eight of the nine first decisions are literally that trade.

This may be a strength — it is thematically coherent and it makes agency personal. It is recorded because it is invisible to CAI-1 and would otherwise reach the openings unexamined.
