## 2. The finding that drives the verdict: the agency kill-gate is being scored a pass when it isn't

Standing rule (`pivot-brief-2026-08-23.md`, repeated in the charter): *"count decisions that change play... Fewer than four is a fail."*

`four-keys-structure-attack-chatgpt-2026-09-01.md`, finding 7, verbatim: *"D1 and D2 may remain lower-order decisions only after their promised consequences are assigned to actual package rows. They cannot count toward the multiple-decisions agency requirement in their present form."* Only D3 and D4 were repaired into real, mechanically-divergent branches. That leaves **two** qualifying decisions, not four.

`prompt-claude-session-kickoff-2026-09-02.md` nonetheless states: *"D3 forks chapter 7 and D4 forks the close, so the agency gate passes."* No document reconciles that claim against the "fewer than four" rule its own attack wrote down. **This lives in `episode-1-structure-v2-four-keys.md`, not the spine.** A prose iteration on the spine cannot resolve it. It needs one of: two more genuinely play-changing decisions added, or an explicit ruling that two mechanically-divergent decisions satisfies the rule's intent, written down with reasoning — not carried forward by assertion.

## 3. Untested against the charter's own genre kill-gates

The six-front adversary prompt at the foot of spine v2.0 tests plot-logic only (the lock, Brad's motive, the room, Margo, chapter 7, the whole). None of the six fronts ask:

- **Cold open ("what happens next" unprompted):** explicitly parked by the kickoff ("stays parked until v2.0 is ruled"). Untested, not passed.
- **Central jeopardy as one sentence with a countdown or closing window:** on the clock in Part A, Violet dies Day 0, Brad's staged death is Day 60, Margo dies Day 214–218 — and **Ally does not start investigating until Day 266**, after every death has already happened. - **Midpoint changes what the player is DOING, not just what they know:** the structure's turn census marks every turn as a belief-change; no beat changes the player's verb.
