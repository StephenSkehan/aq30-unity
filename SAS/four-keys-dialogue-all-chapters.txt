Scene fk_p10_01: The Landlady  [studio, dawn]

  Ally Quinn: Day 326. The morning after the description aired, there was a private message waiting for me. Not through the Tip Line. Sent to me, by name.
  Ally Quinn: A woman four hundred miles away. She lets rooms in her house. She wrote that she had a quiet lodger, under a name I'd never heard.
  Ally Quinn: She'd been listening since the third week. Six weeks of strangers on this show describing one man. Keys moved twice on a table. Thank you, like it costs him. Up at five to run. She said she knew every one of them.
  Ally Quinn: And on his wall, she wrote, there had been a black and gold thing. It hung there until Tuesday. Now there's a pale gap where it was.
  Ally Quinn: She gave me her address. She asked me to send the police, not to come myself, and never to say her name.
