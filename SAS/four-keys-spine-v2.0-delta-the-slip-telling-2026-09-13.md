Two rounds with a fresh Fable agent; round 1 rejected, round 2 accepted with two small consistency edits (mine, marked). ## Round 1 (rejected)

Staged the telling as all four gathered together, swimming, on the slip, with the regulars towelling nearby. Rejected on my own read before it reached Stephen: it requires Liam, Margo and Brad to be swimmers on Day −36, which likely contradicts the rest of the document. Brad is repeatedly staged using Violet's swim time as his window to enter unseen (the tin, the probe); if he is himself in the water alongside her, that method stops making sense. Margo is characterized throughout as deskbound, with no swimming trait anywhere. Sent back rather than folded in.

## Round 2 (accepted, with two edits)

Fable's fix: she tells each of the four separately, in places already established for each of them, over three days rather than one performance. This needs no new trait for anyone and closes off the "any of the four could have talked in front of a bystander" version of the hole almost entirely, at the cost of one honestly-owned exception (Ruby's telling, at the slip, which the document logs as residue rather than hides).

## What this does not resolve (Fable's flags, carried forward, plus mine)

- **Ruby's telling remains the one real exposure.** It is outdoors, seven weeks before anyone asks about it, and recalled rather than proved. The fix does not claim to close this; R20 owns it the way the document owns its other loose threads (compare R1, R6a). A determined adversary can still posit an unnamed listener at the top of the slip; they then have to build that listener into someone who acted on it between Day −35 and Day −29 and stopped the moment the key came in, which is a much more expensive objection than the one this closes.
- **Shed geometry is asserted, not established.** "Nobody within a sentence" assumes the boatsheds are not immediately behind the car park. If they are, this needs one more clause or a different location for Ruby's telling.
- **Margo's office as a place Violet visits is assumed consistent** with her established role as Violet's accountant; if any other document places Violet as never physically visiting Margo's office, this clause needs to move to a phone call (which would then need owning on carrier records, per the document's own standard elsewhere).
- **Not addressed at all:** whoever put the key under the pot did so within view of the lane in principle, same exposure the meter box carried for years. Neither Fable's pass nor mine touches this; it is pre-existing and outside this delta's scope.
- **Pre-existing wrinkle, not introduced by this delta, flagged for awareness only:** Day −36 falls on a Sunday by the document's own clock (Day 0 is the Monday she dies; −35 and −42 are Mondays). The original document has the locksmith cutting four keys on a Sunday. This delta does not touch the locksmith date and does not need to; noting it because nobody else seems to have caught it.
