# 0. THE CARE LINE

Per bible §1.4b. Three plain sentences, no proper nouns, no trade terms, at each level.

**Episode.** A woman died and the city wrote her down as nobody. The man who killed her is living with the woman who rang to say so, and the call cut off before she said where she was. Every day nobody finds out who the dead woman was is another day nobody can find the living one.

**Phase One, leads 1 to 7.** A frightened woman rang for help and cannot be rung back. Nobody knows her name, where she works or who she is with. The only door into her life is the dead woman she called about.

**Phase Two, leads 9 to 13.** A woman is being taken out of the city on Friday by the man she called about. Nobody knows whether she is going willingly. What Ally does this week decides whether anyone reaches her first.

**Phase Three, leads 14 to 16.** A man is nine hours from leaving with the only person who can say what he did. Nobody knows whether a public warning saves her or empties his van. Whichever way Ally goes, the other thing is gone.

# PART B. WHY NO PROFESSIONAL FOUND HER

One causal version chosen for each route. No "may or may not."

| Route | Why it produced nothing |
|---|---|
| **Fingerprints** | Taken post mortem and searched correctly against the databases Havenbay holds. Kerry has no record and never held a role requiring printing. **A correct search of a database she was never in returns nothing** |
| **Dental** | Confirmatory, not searching. It compares a body to a named candidate. There was no candidate |
| **DNA** | Requires a family reference sample. **None was ever collected, because the missing-person report did not suggest anyone was dead** |
| **The interstate report** | Correctly classified low-risk, voluntarily absent: an adult who left a partner, changed her number and chose not to be found. **Under R16 that jurisdiction does not participate in the cross-jurisdictional system Havenbay searches, so the report was never entered into it.** This is the chosen version |
| **Havenbay's own missing-persons search** | Run correctly against local reports. Kerry was never reported missing in Havenbay, because nobody in Havenbay knew her |
| **Maureen's public appeal** | Posted where appeals go, naming no city, because Maureen did not know which coast. **Havenbay never saw it** |
| **The employer** | A no-show casual is deactivated and replaced. That is the procedure and it is not a failure |
| **The landlord** | A cash lodger who stops paying has left. He bags the room and re-lets it. Also not a failure |
| **The camps** | Canvassed. "Not one of ours" from a population with no register removes nothing |

**★ The one fact no professional ever had is her first name.** Every route above needs a candidate and none of them can produce one. **Leah's 41 seconds supply the candidate. That is the entire reason a podcaster gets there and a competent police force did not**, and it is why the episode is fair.

**What it costs Maureen, and it is the theme.** The classification that said *she has probably just left* is the same classification that meant nobody took her mother's DNA. The system was not wrong about the risk. It was wrong about Kerry, and it was wrong in the direction that costs nothing to be wrong in.

# PART G. THE FOUR DECISIONS

Four is the shipping plan. A fifth is held in reserve below in case scripting wants it.

| # | Lead | Class | Choice | Rejoins |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | **A** | Publish that the woman under Stonebridge may be called Kerry, or keep it with Del | 6 |
| 2 | 7 | **B** | Tell Maureen the probable identification now, or verify first | 9 |

**Gate arithmetic:** four decisions · two Class A · one Class C · every branch rejoins within three · no fork opens before the current branch rejoins (3→6, 7→9, 11→14, 15→16). Leads 10 and 14 are shared.

### Decision 1 · lead 3 · Class A · rejoins 6

**Publish the name.** A crowd of listeners is the fastest instrument Ally owns, and someone who knew a Kerry will ring in. It also tells Dean that somebody told, on a day when the only person who could have is standing in his kitchen.
**Keep it with Del.** Leah is protected absolutely and Ally has one route left, the slow one through the appeal.

*Neither is obviously right, and the voice is not on the table.* The caller's audio is never broadcast on either branch. **The choice is about the dead woman's name, which is the thing the whole episode turns on.**

Irreversible: a warning given, or a week of public help that cannot be bought back.

### Decision 2 · lead 7 · Class B · rejoins 9

**Tell Maureen now.** She gives the reference sample immediately and identification moves weeks. She also tells her sister, her employer and her church before anything is confirmed, and none of it can be untold if Ally is wrong.
**Verify first.** Ally gets a second photograph and work corroboration, and Maureen later learns Ally sat on the possibility for three days while she went on paying a dead woman's phone bill.

Irreversible: what Maureen told her family, or Maureen's trust.

### Decision 3 · lead 11 · Class C · rejoins 14

**Official welfare check.** Lawful, immediate, and a uniform at the door. Dean can stand behind Leah while she says she is fine, and then move faster.
**Nova's quiet route.** The under-bridge network can put a message where Leah will see it and nobody else will read it. Doing it burns part of a warning system that people who have no other protection depend on, and it puts a named community contact in front of police attention.

⚠ **Kill Gate Zero on this decision:** Del may act on a credible report. **Del may not create a power for Ally.** Whatever the welfare-check branch produces, it is police doing police work and telling Ally only what may lawfully be stated. See ruling question 4.

Irreversible: the element of surprise, or Nova's network.

### Decision 4 · lead 15 · Class A · rejoins at the close

**Publish his name.** Leah gets a warning she can act on and so does everyone else who has ever been in a room with him. He may abandon the ferry, the van and what is in it.
**Hold for the intercept.** Del stops the vehicle before boarding and recovers the stronger case. Leah spends the last night under his control.

Irreversible: the evidence in the van, or the hours Leah spends not knowing anyone is coming.

### Held in reserve · interview Dean now, or watch him first

Class B, would sit at lead 9. Cut from the shipping four because five decisions in sixteen leads leaves no shared lead between forks. **Add it only if a lead is cut elsewhere.**

### Counterfactual

Remove every player decision and: Kerry is still named, weeks later, by an officer rather than by Ally. Maureen still hears it from a stranger. Nova keeps her network. Dean is on the 07:15. **Named people's outcomes change, in both directions, on every branch.**

### Catch 2. Kill Gate Zero, not one of the twelve, but it kills a decision.

# PART K. RISKS RECORDED

**1. The crime is the most-told homicide pattern there is.** A woman leaves a controlling partner and he kills her. All the freshness sits in the double erasure: the dead woman has no name, the living woman has no address, and each is the key to the other. ChatGPT's audit said that if a spoken test does not strongly prefer this, the premise should be retired rather than repaired a fourth time. **That is correct and it should be honoured.**

**2. ⚠ Representation, and it is urgent because four cases get chosen together.** This episode has two women in danger from one intimate partner. The retained seed from the reader test is an unnamed woman under a viaduct. **If all four episodes are young women killed by partners, we will have reproduced the pattern the genre research names, deliberately and at pitch stage.**

What partly answers it here: the episode's actual subject is **which absences a city treats as urgent**, which is the same critique the research levels at the genre, made into the mechanism rather than the moral. That is a defence of this episode. **It is not a defence of four.** Episodes 2 to 4 should be checked against this one before any of them is developed.

**3. The Tip Line opening is the second one.** The Listener opened on the line too. §2.3.1b rules the device is a signature rather than a liability and that what failed was the absence of jeopardy. **The spoken test decides whether it reads as a sharpened signature or as repetition, and that question should be asked of readers directly.**
