# CHAPTER 9 · ONE OF A KIND · Ally's ninth broadcast week (Days 319 to 325)

Read `00-common.md` first. This file is everything else you may know.

## What Ally holds entering this week

Everything from chapters 2 to 8. Held with the police and never aired: the boat owner at the store door; the skipper's deckhand; the wet boots on the first train. Her private working: she has stopped saying "was" about Brad Collins and has never said so on air. On air she has asked whether anyone saw Brad that Monday morning, and she has the panel from four witnesses in loose descriptions.

## The frame

Four descriptions become one object, and describing it out loud is a decision with a cost. No asset in this chapter may depict the panel itself; the board shows the witnesses' composite, text and fragments, never the object.

## Facts available this week (use only these)

- The four describers gathered: the café owner, the cinema owner, the two signwriters; four memories of one thing nobody has seen since Violet died.
- Cross-check one: black ground, gold capitals, an ampersand. The letters agree.
- Cross-check two: a crack across one corner. Three of four remember it; nobody knows how it got there.
- Cross-check three: a small mark bottom right that was not her name and not a maker's stamp anyone recognised. The strangest fact in the case.
- The juxtaposition (the turn): the woman who judged every hand in town could not place this one, and said so for a month; it was her own alphabet done better than she could do it; and the only other signwriter in her life, her apprentice of seven years, is presumed dead. Ally holds this privately and does not air it. The player holds it too.
- The brush: sable on a handle cut from a broken oar, green tape at the ferrule, hers beyond mistake from across a road. What her hand looked like in the world. Texture, not a clue.
- Del asks Ally, privately: hold one detail back when you describe it, so claims can be tested. She does not say why she asks.
- The description assembled: one of a kind, and about to be spoken into every kitchen in the country, including some Ally cannot know about. Airing it is the only way to find it; airing it warns whoever holds it.

## Decision D4, inside package 08

Air the full description including the mark, or hold the mark at Del's request. Prompt in Ally's voice. Option A label under nine words, flag `aq.fk.d4.mark_aired`; option B label under nine words, flag `aq.fk.d4.mark_held`. Results in Ally's voice: aired means every listener has the whole object and any claim is untestable; held means one detail stays with the police and a true claim can be told from a false one, at the cost of a less complete broadcast.
