# CHAPTER 5 · DID SHE SAY ANYTHING · Ally's fifth broadcast week (Days 291 to 297)

Read `00-common.md` first. This file is everything else you may know.

## What Ally holds entering this week

The public account; chapters 2 to 4: Ruby's room and the letter that cleared the four; the café (money called in, no name); the sister (the house fight, no will, the ashes); the panel Violet carried round the trade; the slip fall; the download map (held); Liam's side, all of it true and innocent; the word "built", held; and one tip held with the police and never aired: a boat owner saw Brad at Violet's store door early on the Monday morning, hours before the four opened it together. Ally has said none of that on air and will not this week. She knows Liam has been the room's answer and Brad was the man who argued against it.

## The frame

Margo Rivera becomes the missing voice. An accountant who kept half the harbour's books; the one of the four who went quiet at the sixth meeting and then withdrew from Ruby; found dead at Kestrel Head five months after Brad, ten minutes early to her own death by the dashboard clock; a partner two hours up the coast nobody in Havenbay knew about. Nobody living can say what she thought. This week Ally maps her last months from the outside and, at its end, asks the city one question on air. The chapter closes on the show's mid-point.

## Facts available this week (use only these)

- Margo's office, shut: accounts for half the harbour, a desk squared to the millimetre.
- A listener writes in: she swam with Margo for the county at fifteen; the woman the town watched stand on the slip with a flask could outswim all three of the others and never corrected anyone.
- How Margo and Violet met (from Ruby and Liam): the undercharged door, the posted cheque, the note that said don't be a martyr. Fourteen years from that.
- The receipts bag: Violet left it every Tuesday for sorting, collected it untouched every Friday, and Margo sorted it herself. It had been a joke once.
- Her last months, from the survivors: after the sixth meeting Margo stopped arguing; after Brad vanished she withdrew from Ruby; she drove somewhere every second Sunday and nobody knew where. A silence with a shape.
- The lane (Day 297, the turn): a listener re-reads the public record. The neighbour at number 14 told police on the day Violet was found that she had seen a man's back in the lane behind Violet's house at about half past ten the night before, going out the back way; a coat; hands empty; no more. The listener sets the neighbour's description of the man's build against Liam, who is a big man, and says: the man in the lane was the wrong build for Liam. Ally airs the comparison as a question and does not resolve it.
- The dashboard clock: Margo was ten minutes early to everything, always ten, and sat in the car until the hour. Her car was found ten minutes early. She went to that car park the way she went everywhere. Ally airs the oddity and does not resolve it.
- The ask: did Margo say anything, to anyone. Ally composes it word for word. Then three days of nothing: asking a city about a private woman gets you her dry cleaner.
- Since the funeral Margo made Ruby cry twice and apologised once: the true thing in the worst way.
