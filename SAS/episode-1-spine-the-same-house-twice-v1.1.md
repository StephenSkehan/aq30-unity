# 0. THE CARE LINE

**Episode.** An eighty-one-year-old woman is already being poisoned by the man who does her shopping, and she is the only person who can authorise the test that would prove it. Nobody can make her, and she is not wrong to refuse. In eleven days the last person who sees her from outside that house stops coming.

**Phase One, leads 1 to 6.** Someone says a woman is being killed and will not put it in writing. Nobody knows whether she is a crank or the only person who has been paying attention. Two people died in that house before.

**Phase Two, leads 7 to 11.** The doctor tells the truth on the first afternoon and it turns out to be worth nothing. Nobody knows how to prove a thing about a living woman without her permission. Getting it costs her something she will not give up.

**Phase Three, leads 12 to 16.** The man does not know the rules changed and that he will be caught after she dies. Nobody knows whether telling him is mercy or surrender. Telling him saves her and closes the other two forever.

# PART G. THE FOUR DECISIONS

★ **All four rebuilt. The audit found one demonstrated decision, two false or reversible trades and one undemonstrated. The false costs are gone.**

| # | Lead | Class | Choice | Rejoins |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | **A** | Use Josie's filed report, or protect her | 7 |
| 2 | 8 | **B** | Send Marion after her mother's records now, or hold her back | 10 |
| 3 | 11 | **C** | Tell Winnie everything, or let Yardley invent a reason to test her | 14 |
| 4 | 15 | **A** | Tell Colin the rules changed, or let Del run it | close |

**Arithmetic:** two Class A, one Class C, rejoins of 3, 2, 3, 1, no fork opens before the current branch rejoins, leads 7, 10 and 14 shared.

### Decision 1 · lead 4 · Class A

**Use it.** Josie's report is dated seven weeks before Ally existed. It is the difference between a podcaster's theory and a professional's recorded concern, and it is what gets Del to listen.
**Protect her.** Nobody outside the agency ever knows she raised it, and Ally has nothing but her own voice.

**Irreversible, and it lands on a named person:** Josie's job and her registration, or the only evidence that this did not start with Ally.

### Decision 2 · lead 8 · Class B

★ **v1.0's stated cost was false and the audit was right: verification does not remove Marion's standing.** The real trade:

**Send her now.** Marion requests Edith's records as next of kin and it moves. **A records request on Edith Larkin is one phone call from Orchard Steps, and Colin hears about it.**
**Hold her back.** Colin gets no warning, and Marion later learns Ally knew for a week and left her ringing about a gas bill. **She is the only person who will still be fighting for Edith in a year.**

**Irreversible:** Colin's warning, or Marion's willingness to fight.

### Decision 3 · lead 11 · Class C

★ **The moral centre, and unlike v1.0's version the two options are genuinely exclusive**, because they are two different ways of getting the same needle into the same arm and only one can happen first.

**Tell Winnie everything.** She is a competent adult and she is entitled to the truth about her own life. She will not believe it, she will tell Colin, and she will be right that she was not consulted about any of it until it was convenient.
**Let Yardley call her in.** He invents a routine review, does not say why, and draws the blood. **It is the second time he has put his name to something he should not, and this time he does it knowing.** It ends him.

**Irreversible:** Winnie's autonomy and any chance Ally had with her, or Yardley's registration and the last three years of his life.

### Decision 4 · lead 15 · Class A

★ **Unique to this premise, and it exists only because of the Part J1 ruling.**

**Tell Colin the rules changed.** He stops. He goes. **Winnie lives.** Edith and Gordon are closed forever, Marion never gets an answer, and Josie lost her job for nothing.
**Let Del run it.** The test, the grave, the pharmacy note and the cameras make a case that answers for all three. **It is slower, and Winnie is in that house while it happens.**

**Neither is obviously right.** One is certainty for the living woman at the price of the dead. The other is a chance at all three at the price of the only one who can still be saved.

**Irreversible:** two deaths never answered for, or the days Winnie spends inside it.

### Counterfactual

Remove every decision: Colin is not warned, Winnie dies in about five weeks, the independent reviewer catches him afterwards, Marion learns what the green lamps were from a court report, and Josie keeps her job. **Named people's outcomes change on every branch, in both directions, and the counterfactual is not the worst outcome on every axis.**
