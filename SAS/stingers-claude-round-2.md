**1 · The Alibi He Never Used**
A man went to prison for killing his wife, and the woman who could have placed him twenty miles away has been paying his mother's rent every month since.

**2 · She Married the Detective**
The woman whose husband was murdered married the officer who investigated it, and he closed the case eleven days before he asked her out.

**3 · He Bought the Boat Back**
A fisherman drowned when his boat went down and his partner sold up and moved away, and last year that partner came back, bought the same boat, and has never taken it out.

**4 · The Child She Raised**
A woman was killed in her own kitchen and her sister raised the baby, and a test twenty years later says the child never belonged to either of them.

**5 · Everyone Left the Wedding Early**
A groom was found dead behind the hall before midnight, all sixty guests had already gone home, and not one of them has ever said why they left.

**6 · He Knew Which Room**
When she was killed the police searched her house for four hours, and her ex-boyfriend rang from another city and told them exactly where to look.

**7 · The Man She Called First**
She rang three people the night she died, two of them told the police, and the third has spent nine years waiting for a knock that never came.

**8 · His Sister Identified Him**
A man was buried under his own name after his sister identified the body, and he was arrested in another state eight years later.

**9 · The Second Widow**
He was killed in his driveway and two women arrived at the hospital claiming to be his wife, and both of them were right.

**10 · She Had Already Changed the Locks**
A woman was strangled in her flat the week she changed the locks, and the only person she gave a new key to says he never collected it.

**11 · The Trial He Attended Daily**
A juror sat through the whole trial, voted to convict, and eleven years later admitted he had known the dead man personally all along.

**12 · Four Men, One Van**
Four men went out in a van and three came back, and each of the three named a different place where they had dropped the fourth.

**13 · He Reported His Own Wife**
A man called the police to report his wife missing and gave them a description of what she was wearing that matched nothing in her wardrobe.

**14 · The Neighbour Who Moved In**
She was killed in her own home, and within a year the neighbour who found her body had bought the house and moved his family into it.

**15 · She Was Wearing His Ring**
A woman was pulled from the harbour wearing a wedding ring that belonged to a man who had been reported missing two years earlier.

**16 · The Best Man's Speech**
A groom killed his wife six weeks after the wedding, and the best man's speech, filmed by forty guests, describes as a joke exactly how he would do it.

**17 · He Was Paid to Wait**
A man was stabbed outside his flat, the taxi booked to collect him waited forty minutes and left, and nobody has ever found out who booked it.

**18 · The Son Who Came Home**
A woman was murdered and her son flew home the next morning from another country, and his ticket was bought three days before she died.

**19 · Two Funerals, One Man**
A man buried his brother in the morning and his neighbour in the afternoon, and both had drowned that week in water they knew well.

**20 · She Left the Dog Behind**
A woman who had never once left her dog overnight was said to have walked out on her family, and the neighbour who broke in a week later found the dog still waiting.
