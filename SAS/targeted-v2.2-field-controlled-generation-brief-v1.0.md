## 4. Non-negotiable hook principles

Every hook must satisfy all of the following before it enters the pooled field:

1. Murder or disappearance is explicit in the first sentence.
2. The reason to care is one plain sentence containing no proper noun and no trade, regulatory or evidentiary term.
3. The audience can understand and argue the central question before learning the evidence mechanism.
4. The stake is one controlled value: **body · freedom · family · identity · present danger**.
5. A living person is dramatically legible before the evidence as a danger, beneficiary, suspect or active protector. Merely existing later in the solution does not qualify.
6. Intricate evidence may support the story and may not be its hook.
7. No murder finding, conviction reversal or decisive identification rests primarily on one fragile small quantitative correction. A corroborated bundle may make a correction credible, but corroboration does not make arithmetic interesting.
8. A plausible wrong hypothesis is present. Ally's public error or visible personal cost is optional.
9. The hook is recognisably true crime to an ordinary reader with no Havenbay context.
10. The hook is not a repair, reskin or recombination of a retired pitch. A portable component may be reused only where it serves a genuinely new human story.
11. The premise must need Havenbay because its setting advances evidence, access, motive, jeopardy or consequence. Fog, docks, old records and civic corruption are insufficient by themselves.
12. A process question is never answered by invention. If a load-bearing Havenbay procedure is unsettled, record **PENDING APPROVAL** and ask the one-line ruling question. Do not kill the hook merely because the ruling is pending.

## 5. Controlled distribution registers

### Inherited situation

Use exactly one primary value per hook:

- wrong person blamed
- culprit or beneficiary protected
- person, identity, body, or evidence missing
- preventable harm ongoing
- keeper, witness, or evidence pressured
- institution continuing harm
- responsibility genuinely unresolved

### Primary payoff

Use exactly one primary value per hook:

- clear or restore
- catch or prove
- find or name
- prevent
- protect
- expose
- understand or decide what becomes public

Classify by effect, not sales language. If removing the named innocent person destroys the investigation's central purpose and reward, the likely situation is **wrong person blamed** and the likely payoff is **clear or restore**.

### Human stake

Use exactly one primary value per hook:

- body
- freedom
- family
- identity
- present danger

### Portable true-crime engine

Use exactly one primary engine and exactly two assigned structural influences per hook:

- **E1 Disputed reconstruction:** one disputed day, route or sequence that the audience can argue before the technical proof arrives. Structural reference: *Serial*.
- **E2 Communal wound:** people broadly knew or suspected, nobody acted, and the place must confront its own silence. Structural reference: *The Teacher's Pet*.
- **E3 Investigation changes the place:** sustained attention makes forgotten witnesses, loyalties or memories surface. Structural reference: *Your Own Backyard*.
- **E4 Repeated action with a face:** a legible living person repeatedly causes or preserves the injustice. Structural reference: *In the Dark*.
- **E5 Naming the victim:** identity, recovery or return is the central mystery and emotional payoff. Structural reference: *Bear Brook*.
- **E6 Escalating living danger:** people around the endangered person recognise the threat before that person fully accepts it. Structural reference: *Dirty John*.
- **E7 Participating subject:** a suspect, beneficiary or accused person engages with the investigation and turns reconstruction into confrontation. Structural reference: *The Jinx*.
- **E8 Legible conflict of interest:** the corrupting incentive is understandable in one sentence before the forensics. Structural reference: *Making a Murderer*.

Structural influence means transferable dramatic architecture. Do not copy a real victim, offender, location, signature, distinctive evidence sequence or recognisable chronology.

10. **Primary payoff:** one controlled value.
11. **Primary engine:** one of the two assigned influences.
12. **Influence transfer:** one short clause for each influence explaining the borrowed structure, never surface facts.
13. **Wrong hypothesis:** one plausible early interpretation and why Ally could reasonably hold it.
14. **Player reconstruction:** the physical activity, the conclusion it enables and the action it unlocks, each in plain language.
15. **Havenbay necessity:** the exact setting dependency. Atmosphere alone fails.
16. **Living consequence:** named living role, operating mechanism and what can worsen now. A proposed character may be described by role rather than named.
17. **Resistance:** the living agent or interest protected by the accepted account and its rational least-cost response.
18. **Process dependency:** the single load-bearing real-world process claim and its primary source, or `PENDING APPROVAL: [one-line Havenbay ruling question]`.
19. **Small-number test:** `NOT DECISIVE`, or state the intuitive non-arithmetic bundle that prevents a small correction carrying the solution.
