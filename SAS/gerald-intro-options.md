# Gerald's Early Introduction — Options for Ruling

**Status:** AWAITING RULING, 2026-08-22. The last open slice of the Gerald-leads-the-tutorial concept: the player currently sees his face on guidance from the L1 payoff onward, but the story has not yet said who he is at that point.

## The discovery that changes the question

**L2, "The Volume-Up", already IS Gerald's lead** ("Gerald has heard every machine in this city ever built. Buy him a drink"). The guided loop runs on exactly that lead. So today's flow is: Gerald's portrait starts coaching you (banners, hints) *while the case that introduces him is the one you are working*. Two readings:

- **Charming**: his guidance is a cold-open; resolving L2 pays off "who is this man who has been helping me."
- **Confusing**: an unnamed old man is talking to you from the UI for several minutes.

## Options

**A — Do nothing (lean into the cold-open).** Zero cost. Risk: F&F confusion about the face. Mitigation within A: the Case File 101 header already says "Gerald's notes."

**B — One-line label on first guidance surface.** The first Gerald banner (guided loop start) carries a one-time name strip: small "GERALD QUINN · retired detective" caption under the portrait, first appearance only. Text-only, no VO, ~30 minutes of work. My recommendation: cheapest fix that fully answers "who is this."

**C — Text-only Gerald beat after the L1 payoff.** A short 2-3 node dialogue (no VO, or Gerald VO later as a v1.2 pickup): Gerald pokes his head into the studio, one line of warmth, one line of craft ("You'll want to keep everything. Evidence has a way of mattering."). Introduces him BEFORE his face starts appearing. Cost: script (Stephen), asset, wiring; breaks the all-nodes-voiced standard until VO is recorded.

**D — v1.2 VO pickup inside L1's close or L2's open.** The full-fiction version, rides the next recording session. Not a b7/b8 item.

## Recommendation

B now (b8 wave), D later if the recording session happens anyway. C only if the cold-open bothers testers in the b7 funnel data.

Ruling needed: A / B / C / D (or B+D).
