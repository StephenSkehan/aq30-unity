The full document, all parts, rebuilt from v1.4 (with amendments v1.5 to v1.8) under the trusting-forgery premise and the three foundations Stephen ruled on 2026-09-11 (`four-keys-foundations-2026-09-11.md`). **The premise in one paragraph.** A woman who trusted four people completely asked them, from behind a padlocked door, to find out who had been getting into her house. She said it was none of them. She had changed her lock and told only the four where the new spare lived, and it had happened again after, so she knew what the arithmetic said, and she wrote down that she would rather be imagining it. Within a month the four were sure it was one of them. Violet never accuses anyone. The accusation is built at Ruby's kitchen table by the four themselves, out of true things, and the true things were chosen by the one of them who killed her, who also wrote one kind line about himself that only one person alive can know is false.

**The three foundations (Stephen, 2026-09-11), which bind every row below.** (1) The van money is modest, unenforceable, and the occasion for Day 0, never the motive. (2) Violet dies because of the mark: the panel is signed with the monogram Brad will live under, and she locked it in a store that opens only on her death. (3) After the second shelf she takes the key in and tells nobody, and she does not watch, because a phone on the shelf would tell her which.

**Status: DRAFT FOR ATTACK.** The sweep (Part C) is written to pass on one named character lock, Margo's, in the shape that passed three rounds under v8.1 (a money lie only she can catch) with the accusation taken out; and on one named character choice, Violet's, which is the premise itself.
