## PASTE FROM HERE

You audited *No Fixed Address v2.1* on 26 August and produced a v3.0 counterproposal. Your central move, naming the dead woman is the means of reaching the living one, was adopted without argument. What is attached is the spine built on it.

Since your audit, three things changed on our side and you should know them before you start.

**One. We moved every rule from before writing to after writing.** For three weeks our gates decided what got written, and forty-five premises produced one survivor that a reader called "artificial and contrived". The same rules applied to a finished draft catch the same defects and cost the prose nothing. Kill Gate Zero, the least-cost test, the access map, the agency gate, the clock convention and evidential language are all unchanged, and they now live here, at draft time. **Nothing you are about to read was generated under a gate.**

**Two. The attached document is a spine, not a pitch.** No prose, no voice, no cold open. Hour-by-hour of what happened, and a ledger of what Ally can lawfully reach, from whom, on which day. This is the step we had never done, and it is why six cold opens by three writers all died on "how did she get that". Judge it as a spine. Do not mark it down for the absence of prose.

**Three. We ran a twelve-objection pre-flight against it before sending it to you.** Every objection is quoted from a real reader rejection. Part H is the answers and Part I is what it caught. **Do not re-derive the pre-flight. Attack what it missed.**

### Your job

**Findings only. Do not write a v4.0. Do not write prose. Do not propose a different premise.** If you conclude the premise should die, say so in one paragraph and stop.

Attack in this order, because this is our own ranking of where it is weakest.

1. **Part I, catch 7, the Maureen joint.** Her missing-person report named the ex-partner and her public appeal was online for eight months, and neither ever reached Havenbay. We give four reasons and each is individually true. **Is the joint sound, or is it four thin reasons standing in a row?** This is the finding we most expect and least want.
2. **Part D, the antagonist's least-cost ledger.** Four dated phases. Find the day on which control stops being cheaper than something else, and say what he does instead.
3. **Part C, the access ledger, and Part C3 in particular.** C3 claims the workplace route is closed to Ally and that naming Kerry is therefore the *only* lawful route to Leah. If there is a fifth route we have not listed, the engine is decorative rather than forced, and that is a kill.
4. **Part G, the four decisions.** Gate arithmetic is stated; check it. Then check the harder thing: is any option obviously correct before the choice, and does any irreversible cost exist because the story needs a cost rather than because it is what would happen?
5. **Part B.** One causal version is now chosen for every identification route. Find the one that still needs a professional to skip a step.

### Rules for your findings

- **A finding must name what breaks.** "This is thin" is a note. "A competent officer does X on day Y, and then lead 6 cannot happen" is a finding.
- **Rank them, and put your own confidence on each.** We will act on the top three and file the rest.
- On your **top three only**, add one line: *what would have to be true for this finding to be wrong.* We have been burned by confident audits in both directions.
- **Where the spine depends on an unsettled Havenbay process, ask for the ruling. Do not kill it and do not invent the answer.** Part J already asks four. Add to that list rather than assuming.
- **Distinguish "wrong" from "not yet demonstrated".** The spine deliberately does not contain a cold open, sixteen written leads, or a merge-board design. Those are later steps, not omissions.

### Two constraints on your output

**No menus.** Do not offer options to choose between. If you have a better version of something, state the one you would ship and why.

**Length.** Findings and ranking. If it runs past four pages you are writing a document instead of an audit.

### One question outside the audit

The episode has two women in danger from the same intimate partner, and this is candidate one of four episodes that get chosen together. Your own research report documents that the genre reproduces an inherited hierarchy of whose absence matters. Our defence is that this episode's subject *is* which absences a city treats as urgent, so the critique is the mechanism rather than the moral.

**Is that a real defence of this episode, or is it the kind of thing a work says about itself?** One paragraph. Answer it separately from the audit.
